GlobalExceptionHandler works for exceptions thrown inside controller methods, but Spring Security handles authentication and authorization errors (401/403) at the filter level—before the request reaches your controllers. That’s why:
GlobalExceptionHandler cannot catch these errors, as they never reach the controller layer.
CustomAuthenticationEntryPoint and CustomAccessDeniedHandler are needed to handle 401/403 errors globally at the filter level, ensuring a JSON error response for unauthenticated/forbidden access.
